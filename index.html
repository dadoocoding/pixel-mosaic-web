Nearest Color matches each square to its closest actual sticker hue. Brightness Ramp ignores hue entirely and colors squares by brightness rank instead (White=lightest ... Blue=darkest) — trades photographic color accuracy for richer tonal shading; best on images with strong midtone gradients rather than flat light-on-dark shapes.

The 6 real stickers are all fully-saturated colors, so dark or muted areas of a photo can end up flattened to one color. Leaving a square's sticker off (showing the cube's own black body) is a common real-world fix.
